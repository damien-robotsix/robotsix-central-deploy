deploy: central-deploy has a Settings panel again, at `/ui/settings`. #622 removed the dashboard's per-service config modal on the correct principle — a component owns its config — but central-deploy is itself a component, and nothing replaced the surface for its *own* settings, so changing them meant hand-editing `/data/config.json` on the fleet host. It now serves the standard `GET`/`PUT /config`, `GET /config/versions` and `POST /config/rollback` over its `LifecycleConfig`, and the page mounts robotsix-ui's shared `mountConfigPanel` rather than a form of its own. Writes are versioned and validated before the file is touched; they take effect on the next restart, which the panel says.
