Caretaker: never auto-deploy when the self-lookup fails. A container destroyed mid-scan could make `inspect_self` return None, which emptied `phase_update`'s self-skip guard and let the caretaker deploy central-deploy over its own container, taking the management plane down with nothing to recreate it. The hostname fallback now lists sparsely so the race cannot fire, and `phase_update` fails closed when the caller cannot identify itself.
