Caretaker health findings now carry the container's recent logs and image reference. The detail was previously just `State: …, Health: …` — a true statement that names no symptom. That text becomes the body of the mill ticket the finding raises, and at ~40 characters it gave the refine stage nothing to build a spec from, so the ticket blocked with nothing anyone could act on. For `mail-ingester` the actual cause — the container printing CLI usage and exiting because it had been registered with no command — was in the first line of its logs the whole time. Log capture is best-effort: an unreadable log degrades to a note rather than losing the finding.
